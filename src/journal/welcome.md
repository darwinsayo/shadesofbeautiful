The old site was a page that said coming soon. So this is the new one.

Mostly it's the photos. Weddings, portraits, kids, church celebrations, and some pictures of the sky when there's nobody to photograph.

I'm going to use this journal for the story behind a few of them. What the light was doing. What I was thinking about. Sometimes what went wrong.

I've been taking pictures for more than twelve years and I still like talking about it. I guess this is where I do that.

If you're thinking about a session, there's a Book a session button at the bottom of every post. Write to me and tell me what you have in mind. I'll write back.

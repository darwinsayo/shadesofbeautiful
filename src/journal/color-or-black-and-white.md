People ask me this a lot. Which one should we get, color or black and white?

Here are two beach photos with the Golden Gate Bridge in the background.

The color one is about the day. The blue sky, the green in the bouquet, the water. You can tell it was a nice day and that everyone was glad to be there.

<figure>
  <img src="/images/wedding-beach.jpg" alt="A bride and groom embracing on a beach with the Golden Gate Bridge behind them" loading="lazy">
  <figcaption>In color, you see the day.</figcaption>
</figure>

The black and white one is about the two of them. Without color, your eye goes to the way they're looking at each other, and then to the bridge. It feels older. A little more serious.

<figure>
  <img src="/images/hero-bw-couple.jpg" alt="A couple walking along a beach with the Golden Gate Bridge behind them, in black and white" loading="lazy">
  <figcaption>In black and white, you see the people.</figcaption>
</figure>

I usually shoot so I can do either, and then I pick whichever one the photo is asking for.

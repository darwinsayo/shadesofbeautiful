I wanted this camera from the day it came out. I liked it because it was completely different from what I was used to. I didn't actually buy it until years later.

I'm trying to pick it up here and there and use it more. I don't have the lens selection I have with the Canon, so a 35mm lives on it full time. That's a focal length I'm not used to shooting on a regular basis, which makes it a good challenge.
